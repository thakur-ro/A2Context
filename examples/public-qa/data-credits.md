# Public QA data credits

The multi-document experiments use the Natural Questions Open questions and answer annotations released by Google and the Natural Questions authors under CC BY-SA 3.0. Liu and colleagues assembled the numbered passages from English Wikipedia. Wikipedia contributors wrote the source articles. Each linked article provides its edit history and contributor credits. [NQ-Open data notice](https://github.com/google-research-datasets/natural-questions/blob/master/nq_open/README.md) · [Data license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) · [Pinned passage dataset and format](https://github.com/nelson-liu/lost-in-the-middle/tree/29b8a6d042ce29abccee3db1a73171a107d7e6af#multi-document-question-answering-data)

NexusWeave selected questions, reordered or substituted passages, ran models, and saved scores and generated answers. Some saved answers reproduce words from the supplied Wikipedia articles. The upstream questions, answer annotations, and excerpts in results, documentation and screenshots retain their source attribution and license. NexusWeave’s Apache-2.0 license applies to its software. The complete passage text used for reruns comes from a separate download. [QA setup](README.md) · [NexusWeave license](../../LICENSE)
